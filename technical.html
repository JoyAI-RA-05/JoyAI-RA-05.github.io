JoyAI-RA 0.5

Scaling Robot Manipulation Learning via Dual Action Alignment

JoyAI-RA 0.5 is a generalist Vision-Language-World-Action framework that turns action-free video and incompatible trajectories into complementary supervision for executable robot control.

JoyAI-RA Framework

JoyAI-RA 0.5 aligns heterogeneous experience through two complementary paths: implicit latent-dynamics alignment and explicit physical-action alignment. It couples visual semantics from the VLM with physical world-dynamics priors from LAC-WM.

A flow-matching action expert then fuses instruction-grounded understanding and learned dynamics features into continuous action chunks for executable robot control.

Human Video Scaling

Robot demonstrations are scarce, while human egocentric videos scale best but usually lack executable actions. JoyAI-RA 0.5 turns this abundant weak supervision into transferable dynamics learning and stronger robot-policy initialization.

Dual Action Alignment

Implicit alignment infers latent actions from visual transitions, allowing action-free human, simulation, and robot videos to train the LAC-WM. Explicit alignment grounds reliable trajectories in a shared physical action space.

Canonical Action Space

Reliable human and robot trajectories are mapped into a 130-D canonical representation with fixed left-hand, right-hand, and body slots, plus camera-frame chunk-relative end-effector actions for cross-embodiment supervision.

Vision–Language–World–Action Model

The VLM encodes task semantics, the frozen LAC-WM supplies learned physical-dynamics priors, and the flow-matching action expert fuses both streams into continuous executable robot actions.

Dual Action Alignment

Semantics tell the robot what, while dynamics tell it how. Because human video scales without executable actions and embodied trajectories carry incompatible action spaces, JoyAI-RA 0.5 routes each data source to the supervision it can reliably provide: latent dynamics or grounded physical actions.

Implicit alignment

LAC-WM pretraining infers latent actions from visual transitions, turning action-free video into supervision for transferable physical dynamics.

Explicit alignment

Cross-embodiment VLWA pretraining maps reliable human and robot trajectories into a shared 130-D action space while the frozen LAC-WM supplies dynamics features.

Physical grounding

Target-robot post-training uses high-quality demonstrations to adapt the VLM and action expert to the robot's observations, kinematics, and control interface.

Continual improvement

Inner-outer loop RL combines fast residual adaptation at the edge with periodic foundation-policy updates at the center.

Real-World AgiBot Benchmark

JoyAI-RA 0.5 is evaluated on six real-world AgiBot G1 scenarios across office, tea room, kitchen, dining table, pharmacy, and dressing table environments. Scores are reported on a 100-point subtask-completion scale and compared with π0.5 under the same protocol.

Human Video Scaling

Human video is not auxiliary; it is a scaling axis. Across controlled studies, more egocentric video expands action diversity, improves learned dynamics, and yields stronger policy initialization with no observed saturation at the largest scale.

LAC-WM pretraining

Scaling EgoLive from 10% to 100% improves seen score from 83.13 to 97.50 and unseen score from 56.88 to 72.40, with robot data and downstream policy training held fixed.

Policy pretraining

Scaling transfers to robot control: seen score improves from 47.8 to 85.6 and unseen score improves from 37.6 to 60.2, with identical robot-action post-training.

Inner–Outer Loop Reinforcement Learning

The inner loop rapidly learns a lightweight residual policy for task-level adaptation, while the asynchronous outer loop incorporates accumulated interaction data into the foundation VLWA and periodically sends an improved model back to the edge.
