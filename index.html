01 / Overview

Heterogeneous experience, aligned twice.

JoyAI-RA 0.5 is a generalist Vision-Language-World-Action framework that turns action-free video and incompatible trajectories into complementary supervision for executable robot control.

WHY IT MATTERS

Robot data is expensive. Human video scales, but it lacks executable actions. Simulation and robot trajectories carry actions, but their embodiments disagree.

See how alignment connects them →
A

Dual action alignment

Two complementary channels route each data source to the supervision it can reliably provide—latent dynamics or grounded physical actions.

B

Vision–Language–World–Action

A VLM contributes task semantics, a frozen LAC-WM contributes physical-dynamics priors, and an action expert converts both into control.

C

Canonical action space

A unified 130-D representation gives heterogeneous embodiments shared semantic slots without forcing incompatible controls to mean the same thing.

D

Human video as a scaling axis

More egocentric video consistently improves dynamics learning and policy initialization, with no observed saturation at the largest tested scale.

02 / Method

Semantics tell the robot what. Dynamics tell it how.

The VLM supplies instruction-grounded understanding. The frozen LAC-WM supplies learned physical-dynamics features. A flow-matching action expert fuses both streams into continuous action chunks.

01Implicit alignment

LAC-WM pretraining

Latent actions inferred from visual transitions turn action-free video into supervision for learning transferable physical dynamics.

02Explicit alignment

Cross-embodiment VLWA pretraining

Reliable human and robot trajectories are mapped into a shared 130-D action space while the frozen LAC-WM supplies dynamics features.

03Physical grounding

Target-robot post-training

High-quality demonstrations adapt the VLM and action expert to the target robot, its observations, kinematics, and control interface.

04Continual improvement

Inner–outer loop RL

Fast residual adaptation at the edge and periodic foundation-policy updates at the center improve task performance at two timescales.

05 / Scaling

Human video is not auxiliary. It is a scaling axis.

Across controlled studies, more egocentric video expands action diversity, improves learned dynamics, and yields a stronger policy initialization—with no observed saturation at the largest scale.

LAC-WM PRETRAINING10% → 100%

More video, stronger dynamics priors.

Seen task score83.13 → 97.50
Unseen task score56.88 → 72.40

Robot data and downstream policy training are held fixed.

POLICY PRETRAINING20K+ h EgoLive

Scaling transfers all the way to robot control.

Seen task score47.8 → 85.6
Unseen task score37.6 → 60.2

All models receive identical robot-action post-training.

06 / Self-improvement

Two loops. Two timescales. One improving policy.

The inner loop rapidly learns a lightweight residual policy for the current task. The asynchronous outer loop incorporates accumulated interaction data into the foundation VLWA and periodically sends an improved model back to the edge.
